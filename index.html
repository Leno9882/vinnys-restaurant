The Legacy

Authentic Sicilian Heart

Nestled just 300 yards from the Boston City Limits and near Sullivan Square Station, Vinny's offers a dining experience rich in authentic Sicilian flavors and traditions.

Our lunch and dinner menus celebrate the heart of Sicilian cuisine, lovingly crafted with the finest ingredients to bring the vibrant tastes of Sicily to your table.

Complement your meal with a selection from our extensive wine list or a cocktail from our bar, and don't forget to indulge in the best desserts this side of the North End.
